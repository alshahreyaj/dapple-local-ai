# Dapple · Google Play listing

## App name

Dapple

## Short description

Private AI tools for photos and audio, processed on your device.

## Full description

Dapple brings practical AI tools for photos and audio to your phone. Choose the tools you want, download them, and work with your files right on your phone.

AVAILABLE NOW

• Background Remover — remove a photo's background and save a transparent PNG.
• Photo Enhancer — make photos four times larger and bring out more detail.
• Object Remover — brush over unwanted objects and fill the selected area.
• Speech Noise Remover — reduce background noise in voice recordings.

Your photos and recordings stay on your phone while the tools work. They aren't uploaded. Once downloaded, tools can work without internet. An internet connection is needed to download tools, which use storage on your phone.

Speech Noise Remover accepts WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC, and OGG files when supported by your phone. Cleaned-up recordings are saved as WAV.

MORE TOOLS ARE ON THE WAY

Document Scanner & OCR, Speech-to-Text, Photo Restoration, Offline Translator, Video Background Remover, and Video Enhancer.

Create more with AI while keeping your photos and recordings on your device.
