# Dapple Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 30, 2026**

## Publisher

Dapple is published by XenEva Studio. Visit [xenevastudio.github.io](https://xenevastudio.github.io/) for information about the publisher.

## Your photos and recordings

Dapple processes the photos and audio you choose on your phone. Speech-to-Text can also record audio while you use its recording screen, after you grant microphone access. The app does not upload your original media or processed results to Dapple. Photos are handled in memory. Recordings and audio results may be kept temporarily in the app's cache until Android clears it or you remove the app. Nothing is saved to your photo library or documents unless you choose Save.

## Tool downloads

When you choose to download a tool, Dapple connects over HTTPS to Hugging Face to download its files. The download service and your network provider may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address, and handle it under their own privacy policies. Your photos and recordings are not included in these download requests. Downloaded tools are stored in Dapple's private app storage and can be removed in Settings or by uninstalling the app.

## Files you select

You choose files using Android's system file and photo pickers. Dapple only reads the files you select for the feature you use. Dapple does not request broad access to your photo library or audio files.

## Data collection

Dapple does not require an account and this app version does not include advertising or analytics features. Model downloads contact Hugging Face as described above. Review the Google Play Data safety section for the app's current disclosures.

## Your choices

You can remove downloaded tools in Settings. You can delete saved photos and audio using your phone's Photos or Files app. Uninstalling Dapple removes its private app files, including downloaded tools and temporary audio results.

## Children

Dapple is not designed specifically for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when Dapple's features or data practices change. For privacy questions, contact the developer using the email address on Dapple's Google Play listing.
